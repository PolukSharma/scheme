# Scheme — video script

Maximum length on Devpost is 3:00. This narration is written to finish by 2:50. Read it once with a timer before recording. If a line runs long, cut the line. Do not add a statistic.

Record against `LIVE_URL` after the team fills that blank. Until then, do not publish the video as a tour of a live site. Use a sample student. Do not put a real STARS report on camera unless that teammate agrees, and do not show a name or an ID.

Wordmark if it appears on a title card: the letters Sc are `#990000`, and the rest of Scheme is `#000000`.

STARS remains the official audit. Say that twice: at the open, and again with the PDF.

## 0:00–0:20 — Problem

**On screen:** Title card, then a catalogue page next to a covered report. No URL typed in the address bar unless `LIVE_URL` is real.

**Say:** Working out whether you can add a minor without an extra semester means reading a STARS report against the catalogue, or waiting for an appointment. Nothing in between ranks the options by extra time. Scheme is a planning aid for that choice. It supports advisors. STARS remains the official audit.

## 0:20–0:45 — Intake

**On screen:** Phone width. Choose a major and a start year. Open a sample student. Do not upload a file in the take.

**Say:** On a phone, enter a major and a start year. You can add a STARS report, type courses, or open a sample student. There is no account. The report is read in the browser and is not uploaded. This take uses a sample student, so nothing was uploaded.

## 0:45–1:20 — Ranked add-ons and detail

**On screen:** The ranked list, then one program. Point at extra terms, courses that already count, and one catalogue link.

**Say:** Add-ons are ranked by extra terms, then by extra units. Open the first one. These completed courses already count. This is what remains. Each requirement links to the catalogue sentence it came from. Pause on that link. The same page is what you would take to an advisor to compare with STARS.

## 1:20–1:45 — Switch and cross-school

**On screen:** The switch list, then one result in another school. Show the alert and the official link. Do not open a page that states an admission chance.

**Say:** The same engine ranks majors you could switch into, against the path you are on now. A different school raises an alert with that school's published transfer requirements and the official link. The alert is not a promise of admission. Scheme does not predict who gets in.

## 1:45–2:15 — Career goal and chat

**On screen:** Type a short goal. Show three suggestions, one quoted reason, and the engine difficulty. Then ask one chat question, and one question about a course that is not in the data. Show the decline.

**Say:** A career goal returns up to three programs. The reason is a quote from the catalogue. The difficulty number comes from the engine. The chat answers from those results and cites the catalogue. If a draft answer names a course that was not supplied, that answer is dropped. Ask about a course outside the data, and Scheme declines instead of inventing one.

## 2:15–2:35 — Term plan and PDF

**On screen:** A term plan. If a course has no printed term, show the line that says the term is unknown. Then the print view.

**Say:** The plan places a course only in a season the record lists. Prerequisites come first. If no term is printed, the plan says the term is unknown instead of inventing a fall or a spring. Print the PDF and bring it to an advisor. STARS remains the official audit.

## 2:35–2:50 — Trust and adoption

**On screen:** The engine and AI split, then the two accuracy figures, then the three adoption phases. End on the wordmark. No prize slide. No team strip in the video.

**Say:** Code decides what counts. AI only explains, and only from those results. On the programs in our accuracy table, 60 of 65 audit cases passed, and 17 of 18 plans were valid. No person has checked those rows yet. It runs on public catalogue data. The path is a USG pilot, then advisors, then an Experience USC embed.

## Read-aloud

The spoken lines, and only those lines, are 366 words. At 150 words a minute that is 2:26. At 140 words a minute it is 2:37. Both are inside the 2:50 ceiling. Hold the ranking and the catalogue link on screen if a section ends early. The demo lead times a full read before recording and cuts a sentence if the clock passes 2:50.
